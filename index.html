Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for macOS users

Is Clipboard x AI free?
The app is free to download. Built-in local tools work with no account. Cloud AI actions use your own provider API key, so you only pay the AI provider directly.
Which macOS versions are supported?
Clipboard x AI requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later, optimized for Apple Silicon (M-series) with Intel support.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Clipboard history and the built-in local tools — extract links, count stats, collapse blank lines, and more — run entirely on your Mac with no internet connection.
Is my clipboard data private?
Your clipboard history stays on your device. Cloud AI is opt-in and sends content only to the provider you configure, using a key stored in your Keychain. We never receive or store your clips.
Which AI providers are supported?
Any OpenAI-compatible provider: DeepSeek, MiniMax, Zhipu GLM, Kimi (Moonshot), Mistral, and local Ollama — plus OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini.
